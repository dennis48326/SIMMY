## 1. Room ids (object data 0x1D) — done

New src/game/roomresolve.cpp + include/simmy/roomresolve.hpp:
- `ResolveRoomID` 800DF228 (the FTilePt as the GC's two words: [0] y, [1] x in 1/16 tiles): the
  tile's room, or for a tile split by a diagonal wall the neighbours on the point's side.
- `UpdateObjectRoomID` = the room part of cXObjectImpl::SetLocation 800DF784: data 0x1D, GlobalDispatch
  0xEF when it changed and data 7 is set (stub: the listeners are the rooms' lights), and the objects in
  its slots (PC: objects whose data 2 is its id).
- `ObjectModuleImpl::UpdateRooms` 800F5B10 (cFixedWorldImpl::ComputeRooms -> GlobalDispatch 0xEC ->
  HouseImpl::DoCommand): every object's room again (level 0 objects forced to 1); stubs:
  cXPersonImpl::UpdateCurrentRoom (overhead lights) and RunTree(7). PC: iterates the module's objects
  by id, because the people the port makes are not in the module's object list.
- `cXObjectImpl::SetLevel` 800E2ACC (was a stub).
Call sites: objectplace.cpp SetLocation (PC stand-in of 800DF784), sanimator2.cpp (the two places the
animator moves the person), world.cpp ComputeRooms (the 0xEC dispatch stub -> UpdateRooms).
Result: the player's sim indoors has data 0x1D = its room; the level's per-room shadow pass draws its
soft blob (outputs/fx_indoor_sim_shadow.png: before/after and |diff| x6 under the feet). The sim is also
now lit with its room's light set (darker indoors: the rooms have no lamp lights yet, see 3).
Not covered: person.cpp's SetLocation (spawn) and objectsim.cpp's PlaceObject / TrySnap (wld agent's
files): a sim teleported into another room keeps the old room until it walks or the rooms are
recomputed; objects put down by sims likewise.
